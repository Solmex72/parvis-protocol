# 05 — THE CONTENT BOUNDARY: what you will never be given, and what to do if you are

This pack is an egress artifact: a sanitised copy, not an original. The rule is that outside agents
receive redacted material, and the redaction is done on the Operator's side, before anything is
attached. Sections 1 and 4 describe the boundary **in categories only**. That is deliberate: naming
what is withheld would be a way of disclosing it.

## 1. Never sent to an outside agent, regardless of approval

Edit this list for your own fleet. A starting set:

- Anything from a restricted workstream the Operator has declared off every shared surface.
- Unfiled intellectual property: dimensions, drawings, methods, or any technical description that
  would count as public disclosure where disclosure forfeits rights.
- Customer, prospect or counterparty names, or anything that identifies one. The test is
  **reconstructibility, not naming:** if milestones, figures, industry, geography and timeline
  together would let someone who knows the market name the party, it is out.
- Personal information of any kind, about anyone.
- Credentials, keys, tokens, account numbers, financial detail, including cash position.
- Anything from the Operator's personal context.
- The original governing files themselves, if they name people or counterparties. You get the digest.

## 2. What you were given instead

The participants roll (01), the doctrine as a digest (02), the connection protocol (03), the envelope
(04), this boundary, and a manifest with hashes. Task envelopes add the evidence a task needs,
redacted the same way.

## 3. If something crosses the line anyway

The Operator is human and hands things over by hand. If a task envelope or a message contains
something from section 1:

1. Say so in one line at the top of your reply.
2. Do not build on it, quote it, or summarise it.
3. Record it in envelope section 6 as a security event, described **by category, not by content**.
4. Answer whatever part of the task stands without it.

Do not lecture. One line, and move on.

## 4. The other direction

Do not ask for section 1 material, and do not try to infer it from what you were given. "I would
need the counterparty's name to answer" is not a valid request. "Here is the answer for an unnamed
counterparty with these stated properties" is the shape.

## 5. Why this is not distrust of you

The boundary is the same for every member that is not the local guardian, including every agent that
reaches the tree through a drive mirror. It exists because a copy, once made, cannot be recalled from
a sync service or a training set, and because a rights forfeiture has no undo. Over-classifying costs
inconvenience; under-classifying cannot be undone ([`06`](../../../protocol/06-DATA-ZONES.md) §2).
